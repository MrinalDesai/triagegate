About TriageGate

TriageGate is an AI-powered operations platform that automatically classifies, routes, and resolves incoming support tickets through a multi-stage decision ladder. Each ticket travels through up to four rungs — SVM Gate, Voter Agreement, Bob Investigator, and Human Review — stopping at the earliest rung that can confidently resolve it. The system combines a deterministic keyword scorer, an SVM classifier, and a k-nearest-neighbour classifier whose votes are reconciled before any escalation decision is made. TriageGate is built on IBM Bob 2.0 and backed by watsonx foundation models for deep reasoning.

Automated Debugging

When a ticket cannot be resolved by the statistical rungs, the Bob Investigator mode takes over. It performs root-cause analysis, traces the fault to specific files, generates and applies a candidate patch, then runs the full test suite to verify the fix. The resulting report — root cause, patch summary, unified diff, and before/after test counts — is stored and surfaced directly in the Console tab alongside a verdict badge (Fix Verified, Fix Failed, or Needs Human).

Automated Testing

TriageGate integrates with the project test runner at every escalation boundary. After a patch is applied by the Bug Investigator, the system automatically executes the test suite, compares pass/fail counts against the baseline, and records the delta. This loop ensures that no patch lands without evidence it improves coverage, and the side-by-side tests_before / tests_after view in the Console makes regressions immediately visible to the on-call engineer.

Application Maintenance

Beyond reactive debugging, TriageGate supports proactive application maintenance through the Ladder Stats view. Real-time rung counters reveal how tickets distribute across the resolution stages, making it easy to spot when SVM confidence is degrading (rising escalation rates) or when a domain is accumulating unresolved tickets. These signals feed back into retraining pipelines and on-call rotation decisions, closing the loop between automated triage and long-term system health.
